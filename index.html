About us

Welcome to Royal Kids Care School

We started Royal Kids Care School in 2019 in Shubham Vihar, Royal City, on Kalwar Road in Manchwa, Jaipur. We are an English-medium school under the RBSE board, with classes from Playgroup to Class 8.

Children can join us in pre-primary and grow with us all the way through upper primary, so we get to know each child, and each family, over many years.

We believe every child learns best when they feel safe, cared for and encouraged. Along with a strong foundation in reading, writing and numbers, we want our students to grow up curious, confident and kind.
