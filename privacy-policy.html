Privacy Policy

How Royal Kids Care School handles your information

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you contact the school and how we use it for admission enquiries, school communication, and related administration.

Effective date: 17 September 2026 Applies to: royalkidscare.eduvizio.com

1. Information we may collect

When you contact Royal Kids Care School by phone, WhatsApp, email, or any admission enquiry channel shown on this website, we may collect information such as:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Student name, class of interest, and age group
  • Message details shared during an admission or school enquiry
  • Basic technical information such as browser or device data collected automatically by website services

2. How we use the information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to admission enquiries and contact requests
  • Share school-related information, schedules, and admission guidance
  • Maintain internal communication and enquiry records
  • Improve the usefulness, safety, and performance of the website
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, or school administration requirements

3. Information sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with authorised school staff, service providers supporting communication or website operation, or when disclosure is required by law.

4. Data storage and protection

We take reasonable steps to keep enquiry and contact information secure and to limit access to authorised persons only. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

5. External links

This website may include links to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps, or email services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, so we encourage you to review them separately.

6. Children's information

Any information related to a student should be provided by a parent or legal guardian. We request that children do not submit personal information directly through website contact channels without parental involvement.

7. Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of contact information previously shared with the school, subject to any record-keeping obligations that may apply.

8. Updates to this policy

Royal Kids Care School may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Revised versions will be posted on this page with the latest effective date.
